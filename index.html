Privacy Policy for Ripple Chat

Effective Date: July 20, 2026

Thank you for choosing Ripple Chat ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience when using our default SMS and messaging application, Ripple Chat (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App. By downloading and using Ripple Chat, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Access

Ripple Chat requires specific permissions to function correctly as an SMS messaging application. The App accesses the following information locally on your device:

  • SMS Messages: We require permission to read, receive, and send SMS messages to provide the core functionality of the App (such as categorizing, displaying, and sending text messages).
  • Contacts: We access your contacts to display the names and profile pictures of the people you are communicating with instead of just showing their phone numbers.
  • Notifications: We require notification access to alert you when a new message arrives.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information accessed by Ripple Chat is used strictly locally on your device to:

  • Function as your default SMS handler.
  • Organize and categorize your messages (e.g., into Personal, Promotional, Finance, and OTP tabs).
  • Display sender names by matching phone numbers with your saved contacts.
  • Notify you of incoming messages.

3. Data Storage and Sharing (No Cloud Syncing)

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, messages, or contacts to any external servers.

All message categorization and processing are done on your device locally. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties because we do not collect it in the first place.

4. Third-Party Services

Since Ripple Chat operates entirely offline and locally on your device for processing messages, it does not integrate with third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising services that would monitor your messaging data.

5. Security

The security of your personal information is important to us. Because your messages and contacts are stored entirely on your device, they are protected by your device’s built-in security features (such as PIN, fingerprint, or face unlock). We encourage you to keep your device secure to protect your personal data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
